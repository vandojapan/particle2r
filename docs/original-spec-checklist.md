## Facing-direction coordinate convention

The documented `進行方向を向く` option aligns the source object's forward
axis with its XY travel vector before adding the configured initial rotation
and spin. AviUtl's built-in triangle points along local `-Y`, while particle
angles use the legacy `(sin(angle), cos(angle))` screen-coordinate basis. The
conversion is therefore `rz = 180° - atan2(dx, dy)`, rather than assigning the
travel angle directly. A direct assignment mirrors the triangle's tip in one
half of a circular path and makes its base face the travel direction.
